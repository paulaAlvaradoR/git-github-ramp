# Here comes the sun

"Here Comes the Sun" is a song by the English rock band The Beatles, written by George Harrison. It was released in 1969 as part of their album *Abbey Road*. The song was inspired by a moment of relief and happiness after a difficult period, reflecting Harrison's joy in stepping away from the band's business meetings.

Source: [Here Comes the Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Here_Comes_the_Sun)

## Album

Album: Abbey Road

# SongFacts

1. George Harrison wrote the song at **Eric Clapton’s house** while taking a break from The Beatles' legal and financial troubles.
2. It is one of The Beatles' **most-streamed** songs and remains highly popular.
3. The track features a **Moog synthesizer**, which was a groundbreaking instrument at the time.
4. Despite its popularity, "Here Comes the Sun" was **never released as a single**.
5. The song symbolizes a **fresh start and optimism**, making it a timeless favorite.
6. NASA used the song to **wake up astronauts** during space missions.
7. The melody and lyrics reflect Harrison's **love for nature** and personal freedom.
8. The song was performed posthumously in George Harrison's honor at **Concert for George (2002)** by Paul McCartney and other musicians.
