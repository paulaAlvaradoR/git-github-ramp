# Smile Like You Mean It
"Smile Like You Mean It" is a song by the American rock band The Killers, featured on their debut studio album *Hot Fuss* (2004). Written by Brandon Flowers, Dave Keuning, Mark Stoermer, and Ronnie Vannucci Jr., the song reflects on nostalgia and the passage of time with a melancholic yet uplifting tone.

# SongFacts

1. "Smile Like You Mean It" was the **fourth single** released from *Hot Fuss*.
2. The song's lyrics capture themes of **nostalgia, regret, and coming of age**.
3. The track became a **fan favorite** and is frequently played at The Killers' live shows.
4. It reached **#15 on the UK Singles Chart**, solidifying the band's early success.
5. The song features **synth-heavy melodies** that were inspired by 1980s new wave music.
6. "Smile Like You Mean It" was used in **TV shows and films**, including *The O.C.*.
7. The music video features **ghostly apparitions**, reflecting the song’s bittersweet lyrics.
8. The song has been covered by several artists, including **Tally Hall** and **David Gray**
