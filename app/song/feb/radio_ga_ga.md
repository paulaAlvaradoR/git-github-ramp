# Radio Ga Ga

"Radio Ga Ga" is a song by the British rock band Queen, released in 1984 as the lead single from their album *The Works*. Written by the band's drummer, Roger Taylor, the song critiques the decline of radio in favor of television and music videos while celebrating its impact on past generations. It became one of Queen's most iconic anthems, known for its audience participation during live performances.

# SongFacts

1. The song was inspired by Roger Taylor's son, who once said "Radio ca-ca," which evolved into "Radio Ga Ga."
2. "Radio Ga Ga" was performed at **Live Aid** in 1985, where the crowd famously clapped along in unison.
3. The music video features clips from the 1927 silent film *Metropolis* by Fritz Lang.
4. The song reached **#1** in 19 countries, though it peaked at **#2** in the UK charts.
5. The title of the song inspired **Lady Gaga's** stage name.
6. Queen performed "Radio Ga Ga" during their final tour with Freddie Mercury in 1986.
7. The song was a response to the increasing dominance of television and MTV over traditional radio.
8. Roger Taylor originally wrote the song on a **Roland Jupiter-8** synthesizer.
